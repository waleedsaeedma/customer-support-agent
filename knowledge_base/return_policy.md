# Return and Refund Policy - TechNL

## Right of withdrawal (14 days)
Under Dutch/EU consumer law, you may return any item within 14 days of delivery for a full refund, no reason needed. The item must be in original condition with all accessories and packaging.

## How to start a return
Contact support with your order number. We will email a prepaid return label. Refunds are processed within 5 business days of us receiving the returned item.

## Non-returnable items
- Devices with physical damage not present at delivery.
- Software licenses or activated warranty extensions.

## Refunds for defective devices
If a device is defective on arrival (DOA), you may request a full refund or free replacement within 30 days, no restocking fee. After 30 days, manufacturer warranty applies instead.

## Refund method
Refunds are issued to the original payment method. iDEAL and card refunds typically take 2-5 business days to appear.
