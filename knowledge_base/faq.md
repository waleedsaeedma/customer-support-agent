# Frequently Asked Questions - TechNL

## What payment methods do you accept?
We accept iDEAL, credit card (Visa, Mastercard), PayPal, and Klarna (pay in 3 installments).

## Do prices include VAT?
Yes, all prices shown include 21% Dutch BTW (VAT).

## Is there a warranty on products?
All laptops and phones come with a minimum 2-year European consumer warranty, as required by Dutch law. Some manufacturers (e.g. Apple, Dell) offer optional extended warranty (AppleCare, Dell ProSupport) at extra cost.

## Can I pick up my order in-store?
We do not have physical stores. All orders are shipped directly.

## How do I track my order?
You receive a tracking link by email (PostNL or DHL) once your order ships.

## Do you sell refurbished devices?
Yes, refurbished laptops and phones are marked "Refurbished - Grade A/B" in the product listing and come with a 1-year warranty instead of 2 years.
